## 振荡电路产生条件

<div style="text-align: center;">
<img src="pic-analog-electronics/Weixin Image_20260407170930_264_5.jpg" width="40%">
</div>

$$ 正反馈电路$$

- 平衡条件：$1-\dot{A}\dot{F}=0$

$$\dot{A}\dot{F}=1，即
\left\{\begin{array}{l}
|\dot{A}\dot{F}|=1\\
\varphi_A+\varphi_f=2n\pi
\end{array}\right.
$$
- 起振条件：$\dot{A}\dot{F}>1$


## RC正弦波振荡电路

<div style="text-align: center;">
<img src="pic-analog-electronics/20211002222519177.png" width="60%">
</div>

$$
\dot{F}=\frac{1}{3+j(\omega RC-\frac{1}{\omega RC})}\\
R_1=R_2=R, C_1=C_2=C\\
\begin{array}{l}
振荡频率：f_o=\frac{1}{2\pi RC}\\
起振条件：\dot{A}>3，即\frac{R_f+R_1}{R_1}>3
\end{array}
$$

## 三角波发生电路

<div style="text-align: center;">
<img src="pic-analog-electronics/Weixin Image_20260407203219_270_5.jpg" width="55%">
</div>

u_{om}=\frac{1}{C}\frac{U_z}{R}T_k$$

## 实用三角波发生电路
<div style="text-align: center;">
<img src="pic-analog-electronics/Weixin Image_20260407203220_271_5.jpg" width="90%">
</div>

$$u_o\cdot \frac{R_2}{R_1+R_2} \pm U_z\cdot\frac{R_1}{R_1+R_2}=0\\
即U_T=\pm \frac{R_1}{R_2}U_z$$

$$T=2T_k\\
\underbrace{2\frac{R_1}{R_2}U_z}_{\Delta U}=\underbrace{\frac{1}{C}\frac{U_z}{R_3}T_k}_{电流积分}$$

## 锯齿波发生电路

<div style="text-align: center;">
<img src="pic-analog-electronics/Weixin Image_20260407203221_272_5.jpg" width="60%">
</div>

$$u_o\cdot \frac{R_2}{R_1+R_2} \pm U_z\cdot\frac{R_1}{R_1+R_2}=0\\
即U_T=\pm \frac{R_1}{R_2}U_z$$

$$T=T_1+T_2\\
\underbrace{2\frac{R_1}{R_2}U_z}_{\Delta U}=\underbrace{\frac{1}{C}\frac{U_z}{R_3+R_{w1}}T_1}_{电流积分}\\
\ \\
\underbrace{2\frac{R_1}{R_2}U_z}_{\Delta U}=\underbrace{\frac{1}{C}\frac{U_z}{R_3+R_{w2}}T_2}_{电流积分}$$
